/faq

Common questions

Is Nyx free?

Yes. This prototype is free and has no ads.

Can I import my playlists?

Yes. Copy the song list from another app and paste it into Library, Import a playlist. Links are not fetched yet.

Where does the music come from?

This prototype plays synthesized previews. Real streaming needs a licensed audio source.

Does Nyx collect my data?

No. Everything stays in your browser.
